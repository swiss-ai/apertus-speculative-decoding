# Per-domain DSpark drafters (8B, canvas A3)

Branch `experiment/domain-drafters-dspark`, registered 2026-11-20 before any
result. The EAGLE twin of this grid runs separately on
`experiment/domain-drafters-eagle`; the traces come from the shared data
campaign on `experiment/domain-drafters-data`.

## Question

How does fine-tuning the drafter on one domain change speculative acceptance
**on that domain**, on **the other domains**, and on the **generalist**
open-perfectblend (OPB) held-out set? Is an equal-budget **mixed** head as good
as each specialist on its own domain, without forgetting?

Earlier evidence: the multilingual adaptation moved EAGLE e10 a lot (+17%
served at k3/C1) but DSpark e10 only +3–5%. That run used a learning rate of
3e-5, low for DSpark (e10 was trained at peak 3e-4), and covered one domain only
([dspark-adaptation.md](dspark-adaptation.md)).

## Design (frozen in `methods/dspark/configs/8b/domain-drafters.json`)

- **Initializer:** DSpark e10 thinking-off. Yu's store copy at
  `/capstor/.../DSpark_Apertus_1.5_8B/..._2026-10-04_epoch10_final`,
  `model.safetensors` sha256 `002d1167…`. This is the hash the adaptation
  repeat recorded, and `rebind` verifies it before training.
- **Heads (8):** e10 untouched (baseline). One head per domain: math, code,
  chat, summarization, multilingual, structured. One mixed head with an equal
  share of every domain (1,333–1,334 rows each). Single seed (1), no setting
  search.
- **Data:** per domain, 8,000 train and 256 dev traces generated by Apertus 1.5
  8B with thinking off (data campaign `MANIFEST.json`). They are hashed into
  `shared/data-lock.json` once; every later step verifies against that lock.
- **Equal budget:** 384 optimizer steps × 2 ranks × 9,344 packed tokens =
  **7.18M input tokens per head**. That is equal tokens, not equal rows: rows
  per step follow each domain's mean length, and the conversion report records
  them. The epoch cap of 4 only lets short-row domains repeat their data. Each
  head is the first checkpoint at global step 384.
- **Learning rate 1e-4:** 19 warmup steps, then half-cosine decay to 0 at step
  384.
  - 1e-4 is a third of e10's peak and 3.3× the repeat.
  - The decay limits end-of-run drift.
  - Everything else follows the e10 recipe: block 8, Markov rank 256,
    confidence head, ce 0.1 / tv 0.9, Muon, 1,024 anchors, target layers
    1/8/15/22/29.

## Measurement

1. **Offline transfer matrix (headline).** Speculators validation `eal_epoch`
   (also accept length and loss) for every head on every domain's 256 dev
   traces and on OPB held-out. OPB held-out is the first 512 rows of Yu's
   `val_ids` split, in corpus order.
   - Each evaluation is one optimizer step at lr 0, then validation. The
     weights are checked bitwise unchanged.
   - The table to read: the diagonal gain over e10, the off-diagonal change,
     and the OPB change.
   - Sanity check: e10 on OPB should reproduce the repeat's 6.269.
2. **Serving confirmation (queued after the matrix):** each head vs e10 on its
   own domain, OPB and one far domain, at k3/k7 × C1/C64.
   - It uses the adaptation repeat's GPU-swapped harness and calibrated greedy
     correctness gate.
   - Decision rule, unchanged: at least 10% more throughput **and** at least
     10% lower median TPOT in both swaps. OPB, TTFT or tail regressions make it
     a conditional tradeoff.

## Gates

Finite losses, real updates (export differs from e10), global step 384,
export reload bitwise equal with config equal to e10 apart from the verifier
path, every lr-0 evaluation bitwise unchanged.

## How it runs

`methods/dspark/train/domain-job.sh` (submitted by
`methods/dspark/train/submit-domain-drafters.sh` → `submit-train.sh
MODE=domain`).

- **Baseline job:** rebinds e10, locks the traces, builds the 7 evaluation sets
  and evaluates e10 on them.
- **Head jobs:** 7 one-node jobs, each `afterok` on the baseline. Each one
  converts its data, trains, exports, backs the head up to
  `/capstor/scratch/cscs/$USER/domain-drafters-backup/` (the iopsstor scratch
  cleaner deleted earlier heads), then evaluates on all 7 sets.
- Every job writes the matrix so far (`domain_drafters.py matrix`).
- A smoke on the data campaign's pilot slice (`domain-drafters-pilot.json`:
  6 steps, math + OPB evaluation, baseline + mixed, debug partition) gates the
  full submission (`AFTER=<smoke job>`).

Runtime: speculators 7046ac6 with the repository patches
(`setup-speculators.sh`), image `vllm_apertus_1.5_release-arm64.sqsh`, side
packages installed by the baseline job into `ROOT/runtime/pydeps`. The cluster
checkout is a `git archive` of the source revision, recorded in
`.source-revision`.

Status and job IDs: `results/8b/domain-drafters-dspark/STATUS.md`.
